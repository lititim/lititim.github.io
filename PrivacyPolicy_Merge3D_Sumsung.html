Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 25, 2026

Developer: lititim

Game: Merge 3D

This Privacy Policy explains how Merge 3D handles data. The game is a free, offline, single-player experience that requires no registration. However, it uses:

  • Unity LevelPlay for ad monetization,
  • Samsung Galaxy Store for in-app purchases, and
  • Unity Analytics for anonymous gameplay analysis.

No personal information (e.g., name, email, contact) is collected by the developer.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Name, email, phone number, or account credentials
  • Precise location, contacts, photos, or files
  • Any personally identifiable information (PII)

2. Data Collected by Third Parties

The following anonymous data may be automatically sent from your device to third-party services:

Purpose Data Collected Third Party
Ad Personalization Android Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, device model, OS version, language, network type Unity LevelPlay
In-App Purchase Processing Product ID, purchase token, transaction status Samsung Galaxy Store
Game Analytics Session duration, level progress, feature usage, device model, OS version, country (from IP) Unity Analytics

Important: All data is sent directly from your device to Unity or Samsung. We (lititim) do not receive, store, or process raw user data. Unity Analytics reports are aggregated and anonymized.

3. How This Data Is Used

  • Unity LevelPlay: To serve relevant ads and prevent fraud.
  • Samsung Galaxy Store: To fulfill purchases and validate transactions.
  • Unity Analytics: To understand how players interact with the game (e.g., which levels are too hard) so we can improve gameplay.

None of these services sell your data to data brokers.

4. Children’s Privacy

Merge 3D is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Your Choices

You can limit data collection on your Samsung device:

  1. To opt out of personalized ads:
    Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads > Enable “Opt out of Ads Personalization”
  2. To reset your advertising ID:
    In the same menu, tap “Delete advertising ID”

Note: Disabling ads personalization will not remove ads but may reduce relevance. Analytics cannot be disabled separately without uninstalling the game.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this policy. The “Last Updated” date above will reflect changes.
