Privacy Policy for Merge 3D

Last updated: January 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you use Merge 3D (the "Game"), developed by independent developer lititim.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Merge 3D is a completely offline, single-player game. We do not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII) from users. The game does not require an account, email, or login to play.

2. Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services that may collect limited data for advertising and analytics purposes:

  • Unity LevelPlay (Ads): Used to display advertisements. Unity may collect non-personal data such as device model, OS version, IP address (for geolocation), and advertising ID (e.g., GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS) to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads via your device settings.
  • Unity Analytics: Used to collect anonymous gameplay data (e.g., level completion rates, session duration) to improve the game experience. No personal or device-identifiable information is linked to this data.

These services are governed by their own privacy policies:
• Unity Privacy Policy

3. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Merge 3D is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us immediately.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
